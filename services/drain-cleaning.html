The Problem

Slow Drains Don't Fix Themselves

A slow-draining sink or tub is usually a sign of buildup further down the line — grease, soap scum, hair, or debris that a plunger or store-bought chemical can't fully clear. Left alone, it tends to get worse, leading to a full backup at the worst possible time.
